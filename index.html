Privacy Policy for Sky Panic!

Effective Date: March 27, 2026

Sky Panic! is operated by Ahmed Orabi ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Sky Panic! mobile game (the "Service").

1. Audience

Sky Panic! is intended for a general audience, including children. We do not require users to create a separate account with us in order to play the game.

2. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, home address, or phone number through the game itself.

However, Sky Panic! uses Google Play services to provide certain features. These services may process limited information required for their operation.

These features may include:

  • Google Play Games Services, such as leaderboards and sign-in functionality
  • Google Play Billing for in-app purchases
  • Google Play platform services for app delivery, security, and core functionality

Depending on the feature used, Google or its services may process information such as:

  • device or app identifiers
  • gameplay-related information such as leaderboard scores
  • Google Play Games profile-related information needed to display leaderboard participation
  • purchase and transaction information for in-app purchases
  • technical and diagnostic information necessary for service operation, fraud prevention, and security

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled through Google Play services may be used to:

  • provide leaderboard and game services features
  • enable sign-in and player identity within Google Play Games Services
  • process in-app purchases through Google Play Billing
  • support app distribution, functionality, security, fraud prevention, and service reliability

4. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to us.

Because the game uses Google Play services, certain information may be processed by Google as necessary to provide features such as leaderboards, sign-in, purchases, app delivery, security, and related platform functionality. Parents and guardians should review Google’s privacy information for more details on how Google handles data.

5. Third-Party Services

Sky Panic! relies on the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Billing

These third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services Terms
  • Google Play Billing

6. Data Storage and Transfer

We do not maintain our own server database for personal user information for Sky Panic!. Any data processed through Google Play services is handled by Google according to Google’s own systems and policies.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to configure the game and its third-party integrations responsibly. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
